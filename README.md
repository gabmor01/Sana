# Sana

Sana is the prototype of an app to manage your ingredients and find recipes to cook with them. The prototype was presented as the final project for the Human Computer Interaction course at Sapienza University of Rome.

The app was developed following the traditional development phases for any app: Conceptualization, Market Analysis, Planning and Design, and Testing.

The app is designed to allow the user to manage their ingredient list, track the expiration of each ingredient, and search for recipes through a social panel where each user is allowed to share their cooking skills with all others.

Link to the final version: https://www.figma.com/file/Kq427G8fyBkhVacc8NRgP6/IUM?type=design&node-id=0%3A1&mode=design&t=PNJdVFdR3jVgGhBr-1

The prototype was presented as the final work of a group (ArmonIUM) of 6 people: Valerio Mesiti, Alessio Taruffi, Gabriele Moretti, Antonio Sacco, Alessandro Pandolfi, and Giacomo Porfiri.
